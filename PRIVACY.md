# Privacy Policy

**Last updated September 28, 2026**

This privacy notice for WL Digital (formerly Minimalistic Watch Faces) ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might collect, store, use and/or share ("process") your information when you use our services ("Services"), such as when you:

- download and use our mobile game **Merge Realms**, on Android or on iPhone and iPad
- download and use our watch faces, such as **Pixel Style Analog Watch Face**, or any other app of ours that links to this privacy notice
- contact us, or engage with us in other related ways

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, contact us at **services.wlsolutions@gmail.com**.

## Summary of key points

**What personal information do we process?** We don't require an account, and we don't run our own servers. Our **watch faces** don't collect personal information. **Merge Realms** shows ads from Google AdMob. On Android it uses Google Play services (purchases, cloud saves, updates); on iPhone and iPad it uses Apple's App Store, iCloud and Game Center. Through AdMob, Google may process information such as your device's identifiers, IP address and device details. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. See [section 1](#1-what-information-do-we-collect).

**Do we process any sensitive personal information?** No.

**Do we receive any information from third parties?** Only what's needed to run the game. For example, Google Play or the App Store tells Merge Realms when a purchase has gone through, so that it can credit it. We don't buy data about you or receive profiles of you.

**How do we process your information?** To provide the Services, show ads that keep Merge Realms free, process purchases, back up your progress, answer you when you contact us, prevent fraud, and comply with the law. See [section 2](#2-how-do-we-process-your-information).

**With whom do we share personal information?** With Google, which provides the ads in Merge Realms and, on Android, its payments and cloud saves; and with Apple, which provides them on iPhone and iPad. See [section 4](#4-when-and-with-whom-do-we-share-your-personal-information).

**What are your rights, and how do you exercise them?** Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising. In Merge Realms, you can change your ad choices at any time under **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices**. For anything else, email us. See [section 8](#8-what-are-your-privacy-rights).

## 1. What information do we collect?

*In short: our watch faces collect nothing. Merge Realms keeps your progress on your device, and Google collects some device and advertising information when the game shows ads. Payments, cloud saves and leaderboards are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone and iPad.*

### Watch faces

Our watch faces run entirely on your watch. They don't collect, store or send personal information. Any data a watch face displays, such as the time, date, battery level, steps or heart rate, comes from your watch and stays on it.

### Merge Realms

**Information stored on your device.** The game saves your progress (score, gems, power-ups, unlocked realms and themes, Quest progress, daily reward streak, whether you bought Remove Ads), your settings and the game in progress, so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

**Advertising (Google AdMob).** To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google's AdMob SDK in the game may collect:

- your device's identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app-set ID; on iPhone and iPad, the identifier for vendors (IDFV). The game doesn't ask for permission to track you, so AdMob doesn't receive your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA)
- your IP address, from which your approximate location (such as your country or region) can be inferred
- device and app information, such as the phone model, operating system version, language and app version
- your interactions with ads, such as which ads you see and tap
- crash and performance information about the ads SDK

Depending on your choices and where you live, this information may be used to show you personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, it is not linked with information from other companies' apps or websites to track you.

**Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store).** Gem packs and Remove Ads are bought through Google Play on Android, or through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, which handle the payment. The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought, so that it can credit your gems or remove ads. We never receive your card or other payment details.

**Cloud saves (Google Play Games).** If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game backs up a copy of your progress to Google Play Games' Saved Games, which is linked to your Google account. This lets you restore your progress on a new device. Google stores this copy, and we can't see it.

**Cloud saves (iCloud).** On iPhone and iPad, the game backs up a copy of your progress to your iCloud account, so it follows you to a new device or a reinstall. Apple stores this copy in your iCloud, and we can't see it. You can turn it off for Merge Realms in your device's iCloud settings.

**Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games and Game Center).** If you're signed in, your best scores and achievements are sent to Google Play Games on Android or to Apple's Game Center on iPhone and iPad, where other players can see your scores next to your player name. Google and Apple handle these, under your account settings with them.

**App updates and ratings.** On Android, the game asks Google Play whether an update is available, and if you choose to rate the game, you are taken to Google Play, which handles your rating and review. On iPhone and iPad, updates come through the App Store, and ratings use Apple's rating prompt, handled by Apple.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the game may occasionally remind you to come back after you haven't played for a while. These reminders are created on your device, and no information is sent to make them.

### Information you give us

If you contact us, including through the feedback option in Merge Realms' Settings (which opens your email app), we receive your email address and whatever you write. Feedback emails from the game also include the game version, your device's make and model, and its Android or iOS version, to help us fix problems. You can edit or remove any of that before sending.

### Diagnostics

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us aggregated crash and performance reports (such as the device model and OS version when an app crashed). On iPhone and iPad, if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us similar crash reports. These reports don't identify you.

### What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, contacts, calendar, photos, precise location, microphone or camera. We don't run our own analytics or tracking tools.

*In short: we process your personal information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.*

- **Consent.** In these regions, Merge Realms asks for your consent, using Google's consent message, before your information is used for personalized advertising or before information is stored on or read from your device for ads. - **Legitimate interests.** To answer your messages, fix bugs, prevent fraud and keep the Services secure, where those interests are not outweighed by your rights and freedoms.
- **Legal obligations.** Where processing is necessary to comply with the law, such as keeping purchase records or responding to lawful requests from authorities.

**If you are located in Canada,** we process your information with your express or implied consent, which you can withdraw at any time, or where the law otherwise permits it without consent (for example, for fraud prevention or to comply with a court order).

## 8. What are your privacy rights?

*In short: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising.*

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada, or a US state with a privacy law), you may have the right to:

- request access to, and a copy of, your personal information
- request correction or deletion of your personal information
- restrict or object to how we process your personal information
- data portability
- withdraw your consent at any time

**Controls you can use yourself:**

- **Ad choices (Merge Realms).** Where the law requires it, you'll see Google's consent or opt-out options, and you can change them at any time under **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices**.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your device's settings (on most Android devices, **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or **Settings → Google → Ads**). On iPhone and iPad, Merge Realms never asks to track you, and you can turn off personalized Apple ads under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising**.
- **Remove Ads.** Buying Remove Ads in Merge Realms turns off banner ads and ads between games. Rewarded ads only show if you choose to watch one.
- **Cloud saves.** On Android, you can sign out of Google Play Games, or delete the game's saved data from your Google account. On iPhone and iPad, you can turn off iCloud for Merge Realms or delete its data under **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**.
- **Notifications.** You can turn off reminders in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

For anything else, email us at services.wlsolutions@gmail.com. We will respond within the time the law requires, and no later than 45 days. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before you withdrew it.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we are processing your personal information unlawfully, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in Switzerland, you can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

## 9. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Some browsers and devices have a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") setting. There is no uniform standard for recognizing DNT signals, so we do not currently respond to them. Instead, you can use the ad controls described in section 8. Where the law requires it, we honor legally recognized opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
