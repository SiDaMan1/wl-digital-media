# Privacy Policy

**Last updated September 30, 2026**

This privacy notice for WL Digital (formerly Minimalistic Watch Faces) ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might collect, store, use and/or share ("process") your information when you use our services ("Services"), such as when you:

- download and use any of our apps, including our games (such as **Merge Realms**, **Wildcatter** and **Lumio: Connect the Dots**) and our watch faces (such as **Pixel Style Analog Watch Face**), on Android, Wear OS, iPhone, iPad or any other platform
- use any other app, website or service of ours that links to this privacy notice
- contact us, or engage with us in other related ways

This notice applies to every app WL Digital publishes, now and in the future. Not every app uses every feature described here. Each app's store listing (the **Data safety** section on Google Play and the **App Privacy** section on the App Store) shows exactly what that app collects.

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, contact us at **services.wlsolutions@gmail.com**.

## Summary of key points

**What personal information do we process?** Very little. We don't require you to create an account with us, and we don't run our own servers or keep databases of our users. Our apps keep your progress and settings on your device. Some apps show ads from Google AdMob, sell in-app purchases through Google Play or the App Store, or offer cloud saves, leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games or Apple's iCloud and Game Center. When they do, Google or Apple may process information such as your device's identifiers, IP address and device details. Our watch faces don't collect personal information. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. See [section 1](#1-what-information-do-we-collect).

**Do we process any sensitive personal information?** No.

**Do we receive any information from third parties?** Only what's needed to run the app. For example, Google Play or the App Store tells an app when a purchase has gone through, so that it can credit it. We don't buy data about you or receive profiles of you.

**How do we process your information?** To provide the Services, show ads that keep our apps free, process purchases, back up your progress, answer you when you contact us, prevent fraud, and comply with the law. See [section 2](#2-how-do-we-process-your-information).

**With whom do we share personal information?** With Google and Apple, which provide the ads, payments, cloud saves, leaderboards and app distribution our apps use. See [section 4](#4-when-and-with-whom-do-we-share-your-personal-information).

**What are your rights, and how do you exercise them?** Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising. In any of our apps that shows ads, you can change your ad choices at any time under **Ad privacy choices** in the app's Settings. For anything else, email us. See [section 8](#8-what-are-your-privacy-rights).

## 1. What information do we collect?

*In short: our apps keep your progress and settings on your device. Apps that show ads, sell in-app purchases or offer cloud saves and leaderboards use Google's and Apple's services for those features, and Google or Apple may collect some device and account information to provide them. Our watch faces collect nothing.*

The features below are described once for all our apps. An app only collects the information for the features it actually has.

### Information stored on your device

Our apps save your progress (for example, levels, scores, in-game currency and items, unlocks, streaks and achievements), your settings, and which in-app purchases you own, on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling an app or clearing its data deletes it. On iPhone and iPad, it may also be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, if you use one.

### Watch faces and device sensors

Our watch faces run entirely on your watch. They don't collect, store or send personal information. Any data a watch face or app displays from your device, such as the time, date, battery level, weather, steps or heart rate, comes from your device and stays on it.

### Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some of our apps show ads from Google AdMob, such as banners, ads between games, or video ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google's AdMob SDK may collect:

- your device's identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app-set ID; on iPhone and iPad, the identifier for vendors (IDFV). Our apps don't ask for permission to track you, so AdMob doesn't receive your iPhone's or iPad's advertising identifier (IDFA)
- your IP address, from which your approximate location (such as your country or region) can be inferred
- device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language and app version
- your interactions with ads, such as which ads you see and tap
- crash and performance information about the ads SDK

Depending on your choices and where you live, this information may be used to show you personalized ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, using Google's consent message, and you can change your choices at any time under **Ad privacy choices** in the app's Settings. On iPhone and iPad, this information is not linked with information from other companies' apps or websites to track you.

### Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store)

Some of our apps sell in-app purchases, such as in-game currency, removing ads, or optional tips. These are bought through Google Play on Android, or through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, which handle the payment. The app receives only a confirmation of what you bought and which products you own, so that it can credit your purchase and restore it on a new device. We never receive your card or other payment details.

### Cloud saves (Google Play Games and iCloud)

Some of our apps back up a copy of your progress to the cloud, so it follows you to a new device or a reinstall. On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, this copy is stored in Google Play Games' Saved Games, linked to your Google account. On iPhone and iPad, it is stored in your iCloud account. Google or Apple stores this copy, and we can't see it.

### Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games and Game Center)

Some of our apps have leaderboards and achievements. If you're signed in, your scores (or other results, such as a net worth) and achievements are sent to Google Play Games on Android or to Apple's Game Center on iPhone and iPad, where other players can see your scores next to your player name. Google and Apple handle these, under your account settings with them.

### App updates and ratings

On Android, an app may ask Google Play whether an update is available, and if you choose to rate an app, you are taken to Google Play, which handles your rating and review. On iPhone and iPad, updates come through the App Store, and ratings use Apple's rating prompt, handled by Apple.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, an app may send you reminders, for example when a daily reward is waiting, when something in the game needs your attention, or after you haven't played for a while. These reminders are worked out and scheduled on your device, and no information is sent to make them. You can turn them off in the app's Settings, where available, or in your device's notification settings.

### Information you give us

If you contact us, including through a feedback or bug report option in one of our apps (which opens your email app), we receive your email address and whatever you write. Feedback emails from an app may also include the app version, your device's make and model, and its operating system version, to help us fix problems. You can edit or remove any of that before sending.

### Diagnostics

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us aggregated crash and performance reports (such as the device model and OS version when an app crashed). On iPhone and iPad, if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us similar crash reports. These reports don't identify you.

### What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, contacts, calendar, photos, precise location, microphone or camera. We don't run our own analytics or tracking tools, and we don't collect information for any purpose not described in this notice. If an app ever needs a device permission for a feature (for example, a watch face reading your step count), the information stays on your device unless this notice says otherwise.

*In short: we process your personal information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.*

**If you are located in the EU, UK or Switzerland,** we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Consent.** In these regions, our apps that show ads ask for your consent, using Google's consent message, before your information is used for personalized advertising or before information is stored on or read from your device for ads. - **Legitimate interests.** To answer your messages, fix bugs, prevent fraud and keep the Services secure, where those interests are not outweighed by your rights and freedoms.
- **Legal obligations.** Where processing is necessary to comply with the law, such as keeping purchase records or responding to lawful requests from authorities.

**If you are located in Canada,** we process your information with your express or implied consent, which you can withdraw at any time, or where the law otherwise permits it without consent (for example, for fraud prevention or to comply with a court order).

## 8. What are your privacy rights?

*In short: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising.*

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada, or a US state with a privacy law), you may have the right to:

- request access to, and a copy of, your personal information
- request correction or deletion of your personal information
- restrict or object to how we process your personal information
- data portability
- withdraw your consent at any time

**Controls you can use yourself:**

- **Ad choices.** In our apps that show ads, where the law requires it, you'll see Google's consent or opt-out options, and you can change them at any time under **Ad privacy choices** in the app's Settings.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your device's settings (on most Android devices, **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or **Settings → Google → Ads**). On iPhone and iPad, our apps never ask to track you, and you can turn off personalized Apple ads under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising**.
- **Removing ads.** Where an app offers a purchase that removes ads, buying it turns off banner ads and ads between games. Ads that give you a reward only show if you choose to watch one.
- **Cloud saves.** On Android, you can sign out of Google Play Games, or delete an app's saved data from your Google account. On iPhone and iPad, you can turn off iCloud for an app or delete its data under **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**.
- **Notifications.** You can turn off reminders in the app's Settings, where available, or in your device's notification settings.

For anything else, email us at services.wlsolutions@gmail.com. We will respond within the time the law requires, and no later than 45 days. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before you withdrew it.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we are processing your personal information unlawfully, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in Switzerland, you can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

## 9. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Some browsers and devices have a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") setting. There is no uniform standard for recognizing DNT signals, so we do not currently respond to them. Instead, you can use the ad controls described in section 8. Where the law requires it, we honor legally recognized opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
