# Merge Realms support

**Merge Realms** is a fantasy merge puzzle game by **WL Digital**, for iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Contact us

Email **services.wlsolutions@gmail.com** with questions, bug reports or suggestions. We usually
reply within two working days. You can also write to us from inside the game: **Settings → Feedback →
Report a bug** or **Suggest**, which opens your email app with your game version and device
filled in.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Just open the game and play. On iPhone and iPad your progress is backed up to iCloud with
your Apple ID, so it follows you to a new device. On Android it's backed up with Google Play
Games.

**I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?**
Sign in with the same Apple ID (or Google account on Android) and open the game: Remove Ads and
the Launch Bundle are restored automatically at launch. Your gem balance comes with your cloud
save.

**How do I stop personalised ads?**
In the game, go to **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices**. Buying Remove Ads removes the
banner ads and the ads between games; optional ads you choose to watch for rewards stay
available.

**Where are the leaderboards and achievements?**
They use Game Center on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play Games on Android. The rankings open
from the leaderboard panel on the Classic tab, and achievements are on the Goals tab.

**How do I play?**
Drag relics onto the board and match two of a kind to merge them into the next one up, from a
Spark all the way to a Dragon. Move relics anywhere for free, cover sprouts before they grow
into brambles, and merge two Dragons for an Ascension that sweeps the board. You can replay the
tutorial any time from **Settings → Help & News → Replay tutorial**.
